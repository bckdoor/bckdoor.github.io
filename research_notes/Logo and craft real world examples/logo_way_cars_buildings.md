# The "Logo Way" Applied to Cars, Buildings, Stores, Hotels, Branded Real Estate and Offices

Research note for the thesis "Gesamtkunstwerk without totalising", covering side (1), the logo way. Compiled October 2026.

**Method caveat on photos and licenses:** commons.wikimedia.org could not be fetched directly from this environment (DNS and proxy blocked). The Commons file and category URLs below come from search results and are real pages. Their **licenses could not be read**, so each one is marked "license: verify on page". As a rule of thumb, files whose names end in a Flickr ID such as `(49570496372)` were bulk-imported from Flickr. These are usually CC BY or CC BY-SA, but they must be checked one by one. No license has been assumed.

**Scale used in the classifications:** LOGO-HEAVY means the monogram or mark itself is the surface. LOGO-LIGHT means brand "codes" or patterns without an overt mark. CRAFT-LEANING means the material, leather and workmanship are the message.

## 1. Cars dressed in fashion logos (scene: car interior)

### Takeaway
There is a continuous 50-year lineage, from the Gucci-trimmed AMC Hornet (1972) to the Louis Vuitton x Singer Porsche 911s (2025). It moves from striped upholstery and crests to monogram-perforated seats. Hermès (Bugatti) is the clearest craft-leaning counterexample. Versace (Murciélago) and LV x Singer are the most logo-heavy.

### Cited Findings
**1972–73 AMC Hornet Sportabout "Gucci" (Gucci X).** Brand: Gucci. Scene: car interior.
- AMC sold this as a $141.75 designer package on the Hornet Sportabout wagon. It added green carpet and Gucci-stripe upholstery in trademark colours, and 4,835 were sold in 1972–73 — [Hagerty, "Lipstick on a Hornet"](https://hagerty.com/media/?p=329702); [Hagerty, "When Gucci-trimmed cars had automakers seeing green"](https://hagerty.com/media/?p=4356)
- One source says the package added the Gucci family crest to the exterior, and that Aldo Gucci was reportedly unhappy that a family wagon carried the double G — per the search summary of the [Hagerty pieces](https://hagerty.com/media/?p=4356) (treat the crest detail as single-source)
- Commons: a search found **no Gucci-Hornet file**. The Hornet category has a related designer-option car, the "1973 AMC Hornet X with Levis option" ([File page](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:1973_AMC_Hornet_X_with_Levis_option_at_AMO_2015_meet-1of4.jpg), license: verify on page). This is an analogous "fashion-trim" car (Levi's denim-look interior), but it is not Gucci.

**1979 Cadillac Seville "Gucci edition".** Brand: Gucci. Scene: car interior/exterior.
- This was a coachbuilt limited run by International Automotive Design, Miami, sold only through one Miami dealer. It cost $19,900, which made it the most expensive Cadillac available. It had Gucci branding throughout, including gold-plated GG logos, and an estimated 200–300 were built. Aldo Gucci reportedly debuted it at the Fontainebleau Miami in 1978. The sources differ on 1978 vs 1979 — [The Truth About Cars, "Rare Rides: A Cadillac Seville Gucci"](https://thetruthaboutcars.com/cars/features/rare-rides-a-cadillac-seville-gucci-forever-florida-from-1979-45135370); [Hagerty](https://hagerty.com/media/?p=4356)
- Lincoln reportedly declined a Gucci Mark IV, which pushed Aldo Gucci to Cadillac — [Hagerty](https://hagerty.com/media/?p=4356)
- An overview of trunk/bag-brand special editions (Louis Vuitton, Gucci, Hermès, MCM, Etienne Aigner) appears at [Auto Bild](https://www.autobild.de/artikel/luxus-sondermodelle-die-autos-der-koffer-und-taschenmarken-louis-vuitton-gucci-hermes-mcm-etienne-aigner-28769295.html), which is useful for further examples
- Commons: no file found.

**Fiat 500 by Gucci (2011–, incl. 500C convertible).** Brand: Gucci. Scene: car interior/exterior. LOGO-HEAVY: green-red-green web stripe body band, GG on seats and wheels.
- Commons has a dedicated category with about 8 files, listed under Fiat 500 (2007) special editions — [Category:Fiat 500 by Gucci](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Fiat_500_by_Gucci)

**Mercedes-Benz Project Geländewagen (G-Class), Project MAYBACH (2021) and "Maybach by Virgil Abloh" S 680 (2022).** Brand: Mercedes-Maybach x Virgil Abloh / Off-White. Scene: car exterior/interior. LOGO-LIGHT to MEDIUM: two-tone black/sand finish, co-branded accessories and an Off-White capsule. It is a designer collab rather than a monogram wrap.
- A limited-edition S 680 built by Mercedes-Benz MANUFAKTUR, Sindelfingen. Its two-tone black and sand finish carries over from Project MAYBACH. It is Abloh's third Mercedes project, after Project Geländewagen — [Mercedes-Benz USA media release](https://media.mbusa.com/releases/virgil-abloh-and-mercedes-maybach-create-the-ultimate-legacy-car)

An Off-White capsule collection was sold alongside — [V Magazine](https://vmagazine.com/?p=228612); [Automotive World](https://www.automotiveworld.com/news/virgil-abloh-and-mercedes-maybach-create-the-ultimate-legacy-car/)

**Louis Vuitton x Singer, two Porsche 911 "Reimagined by Singer" (Monterey Car Week; reported August 2025, exact year to verify).** Brand: Louis Vuitton. Scene: CAR INTERIOR, the best current LV example. LOGO-HEAVY: Monogram flowers are cut into the seat ventilation holes, malletage (trunk-lining) quilting runs over the seats, front boot and engine bay, and the livery uses LV house colours.
- Two one-offs. One is a Classic in saffron, cobalt blue and yellow. The other is a Classic Turbo cabriolet in "Calcaire Lutétien" white, conceived by Nicolas Ghesquière. The Monogram flower motif is used on seat perforations and the trims are quilted after the trunks. The cars have a removable Tambour-style dashboard clock from La Fabrique du Temps Louis Vuitton (it doubles as a desk clock), and the door-handle edges are dyed like LV leather goods. This is reported as LV's first co-branded automotive collaboration — [CarExpert](https://www.carexpert.com.au/car-news/singer-and-louis-vuitton-reimagine-the-porsche-911-for-monterey-car-week); [Galerie Magazine](https://galeriemagazine.com/louis-vuitton-and-singer-unveil-two-exclusive-porsche-911s/); [Europa Star](https://www.europastar.com/time-keeper/1004116343-louis-vuitton-takes-the-road-with-singer.html); [Man of Many](https://manofmany.com/auto/cars/louis-vuitton-singer-custom-porsche-911)
- Commons: none found.

**Historic LV car trunks (c.1910).** Brand: Louis Vuitton. Scene: car / travel. Bespoke monogram-canvas trunks were made to order to fit a specific car's boot or rear rack — [Vinterior listing](https://vinterior.co/furniture/storage/trunks/rare-20th-century-louis-vuitton-car-trunk-in-monogram-canvas-france-c-1910-sku32073757). Commons has historic trunk images, e.g. [File:Malle Vuitton-1923.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Malle_Vuitton-1923.jpg) (a 1923 advert, likely public domain by age; verify).

**Lamborghini Murciélago LP640 Versace (2006).** Brand: Versace. Scene: car interior. LOGO-HEAVY: Greek-fret (Greca) pattern on most of the upholstery.
- Revealed at the 2006 Paris Motor Show. Ten cars were built, with two paint choices and Greek Fret upholstery — [Supercars.net](https://www.supercars.net/blog/?p=226187)
- Commons: none found.

**Bugatti Veyron 16.4 "Fbg par Hermès" (2008).** Brand: Hermès. Scene: car interior. **CRAFT-LEANING / hybrid.** Hermès leather, saddle-stitch and the "H" motif in the grille. This is a materials-led collaboration, so it belongs on the thesis's side (2) or as a boundary case, not as a pure logo example.
- Unveiled at the 2008 Geneva Motor Show and designed by Gabriele Pezzini. It cost €1.55 million before tax, with two-tone ebony/étoupe or ebony/brick colourways — [Bugatti Newsroom press release](https://newsroom.bugatti.com/press-releases/the-bugatti-veyron-fbg-par-herm-s-); [Supercars.net](https://www.supercars.net/blog/2008-bugatti-164-veyron-fbg-par-hermes)
- Commons: none found.

### Inferences
- For a "car interior" photo with a free licence, the best bets are the **Fiat 500 by Gucci** Commons files (GG seats and stripe) and the **Maybach by Virgil Abloh** Commons file. LV x Singer is the strongest LV example but appears to have only press imagery, so ask Singer/LV for press use.
- The arc runs from trim package (AMC), through coachbuilt luxury (Cadillac), to designer-as-artist (Abloh), to the house as total author (LV x Singer, where even the clock belongs to the house). That progression illustrates "totalising" well.

### Gaps
- No verified sources this session for: Fendi Fiat 500 (I found no evidence it exists; possibly confused with "Fiat 500 by Gucci" or Fendi x Maserati), Trussardi-edition cars, a Bentley/Mulliner fashion collab, or a Rolls-Royce with a fashion-house trim. Bentley and Rolls-Royce bespoke work is generally craft/material-led, not logo-led.
- No verified example of a factory or LV-sanctioned **LV monogram car wrap**. Online monogram wraps appear to be aftermarket or unofficial and were not sourced here, so they should be treated as unverified and possibly counterfeit-use.
- Licenses for all Commons files are unverified (Commons was unreachable).
- The exact reveal year of LV x Singer is not confirmed from a fetched page. The article ecosystem suggests August 2025.

## 2. Stores and buildings wrapped in the monogram (scene: house/home exterior, city)

### Takeaway
LV's architecture moves from monogram-patterned facades (Aoki in Tokyo; Matsuya Ginza in 2013–14) to the literal building-as-object: the giant monogram trunk hoarding on the Champs-Élysées (from late 2023) and the monogram-hulled ship "The Louis" in Shanghai (June 2025). Gehry's Maison Seoul (2019) is logo-light, a sculptural rather than monogram building. Prada Marfa (2005) is the art-world critique of the boutique as logo-object.

### Cited Findings
**Louis Vuitton trunk facade, 103–111 avenue des Champs-Élysées, Paris (installed late 2023/Jan 2024; still reported early 2026).** Scene: building exterior. LOGO-HEAVY to the extreme: the whole building is hidden in a wood-and-aluminium replica of a monogram trunk.
- The entire building is covered by a wood and aluminium installation replicating the monogram trunk, to conceal the works for LV's first hotel — [Domus (15 Jan 2024)](https://domusweb.it/en/news/2024/01/15/louis-vuitton-opens-its-first-hotel-in-paris-and-while-waiting-conceals-the-construction-site-inside-one-of-its-trunks.amp.html)
- Paris's commercial planning commission approved the project on 28 Sept 2023, and an opening is expected in 2026. Coverage from February 2026 still describes the hotel as forthcoming, with about ten suites, not confirmed — [Sortir à Paris](https://www.sortiraparis.com/en/where-to-eat-in-paris/hotel/articles/286591-the-first-louis-vuitton-hotel-on-the-champs-elysees); [Hospitality-ON](https://hospitality-on.com/en/ouverture/louis-vuitton-plans-open-its-first-luxury-hotel-champs-elysees)
- Environmental groups and Paris elected officials challenged the permit in the Paris administrative court as illegal advertising on a historic building. One report said it would stay until 2027 — [Le Bonbon](https://en.lebonbon.fr/paris/news/louis-vuitton-champs-elysees-bag/); [Luxus Plus](https://luxus-plus.com/en/champs-elysees-environmentalists-call-on-louis-vuitton-to-remove-its-giant-trunk/). **This makes it a direct, documented public controversy over the logo-as-architecture, useful for the thesis.**
- The site of about 22,000 m² was previously meant for Dior's HQ — [Luxury Travel Magazine](https://www.luxurytravelmag.com.au/?p=32091)
- Related: LV's New York store hoarding was stacked with giant LV trunks (Nov 2024) — [Style on the Dot](https://styleonthedot.com/2024/11/08/piled-up-with-lv-trunks/)
- Commons: no file of the trunk found by search. Neighbouring LV flagship (101 Champs-Élysées) files exist: [Category:Louis Vuitton Maison Champs Élysées](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Louis_Vuitton_Maison_Champs_%C3%89lys%C3%A9es); [File:Louis Vuitton Maison Champs Élysées (49570496372).jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Louis_Vuitton_Maison_Champs_%C3%89lys%C3%A9es_(49570496372).jpg) (Flickr import; license: verify). Recommendation: browse the category for 2024–25 uploads showing the trunk.
- Status as of Oct 2026 (removed, or hotel opened) is unverified.

**"The Louis", Louis Vuitton Shanghai (opened late June/July 2025).** Scene: building exterior. LOGO-HEAVY: a ship-shaped building about 30 m tall with a metallic Monogram-patterned hull and upper levels evoking stacked trunks.
- About 1,600 m² over three levels, combining retail, Le Café Louis Vuitton and the exhibition "Visionary Journeys" (designed by Shohei Shigematsu/OMA). It sits in Jing'an near Taikoo Hui (Nanjing West Road) and refers to trunk-making for ocean travel and to Shanghai's port — [FashionUnited (30 Jun 2025)](https://fashionunited.com/news/retail/louis-vuitton-presents-the-louis-in-shanghai/2025063066889); [China Daily](https://regional.chinadaily.com.cn/guangxi/2025-07/07/c_1106352.htm); [Globetrender](https://globetrender.com/2025/07/11/louis-vuitton-opens-vessel-shaped-flagship-store-shanghai/); [Inside Retail](https://insideretail.com.au/sectors/louis-vuitton-opens-ship-inspired-concept-store-in-shanghai-202506)
- Commons: not searched successfully; likely press images only.

**Louis Vuitton Maison Seoul, Cheongdam-dong (opened 30 Oct 2019).** Architect: Frank Gehry (exterior); Peter Marino (interiors). Scene: building exterior. **LOGO-LIGHT**: the glass "sails" echo Gehry's Fondation LV and Korean references (Hwaseong Fortress, the Dongnae Hakchum crane dance). There is no monogram cladding.
- Gehry's first Korean building. The top floor has Espace Louis Vuitton (opening show of Giacometti) — [Inside Retail Asia](https://insideretail.asia/2019/11/05/louis-vuitton-seoul-flagship-opens/); [Metalocus](https://www.metalocus.es/en/news/frank-gehry-designs-a-miniature-fondation-louis-vuitton-seoul); [Archilovers](https://archilovers.com/projects/260825/louis-vuitton-maison-seoul.html)
- Commons: no file found by search (the Category:Louis Vuitton top-level page may hold one; check [Category:Louis Vuitton](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Louis_Vuitton)).

**Louis Vuitton Ginza Namiki, Tokyo (2021), by Jun Aoki & Associates (exterior), Peter Marino (interior).** Scene: building exterior. **Correction to brief: LOGO-LIGHT.** The 2021 facade is rippling double-layered glass with dichroic film, "like water". Dezeen calls it "Louis Vuitton Tokyo"; it is likely the Matsuya Ginza store, 2013–14 (store name to verify) — [Dezeen (3 Oct 2014)](https://www.dezeen.com/2014/10/03/aoki-jun-associates-louis-vuitton-tokyo-perforated-monogrammed-facade/amp/). **This one fits the logo way.**

**Louis Vuitton Omotesando, Tokyo (2002), by Jun Aoki.** Scene: building exterior. Stacked "trunk" volumes (a design intent widely reported, not re-verified this session).

### Inferences
- The Champs-Élysées trunk is the strongest single image for "the logo way at architectural scale". The building literally becomes the product, and the legal challenge calling it "advertising" sharpens the thesis's argument.
- The Gehry and Aoki 2021 buildings show LV itself oscillating toward a logo-light, material/sculptural approach. That is useful nuance, since even within LV the totalising logo is a choice, not a necessity.

### Gaps
- No freely licensed photo confirmed for the Champs-Élysées trunk, The Louis or Maison Seoul.
- The exact name and year of Aoki's 2014 monogram-perforated store are unconfirmed (likely LV Matsuya Ginza).
- LV Roppongi (Aoki, 2003, with a glass-tube facade) was not researched.

## 3. Branded residences and hotels (scene: living / home)

### Takeaway
Fashion-branded residences are overwhelmingly **logo-light in architecture and logo-medium inside**. The brand supplies furniture, finishes and "codes" (Armani/Casa, Fendi Casa, Versace Home) rather than plastering a monogram. Palazzo Versace (Gold Coast 2000, Dubai 2015) is the most logo-saturated, with Medusa heads and Greca throughout. Auto-branded towers (Porsche Design, Bentley) sell the car itself as the domestic feature.

### Cited Findings
**Palazzo Versace, Gold Coast, Australia (opened 2000).** Scene: living/hotel. LOGO-HEAVY (Medusa, Greca, Versace Home furnishings throughout).
- A luxury hotel on the Southport Spit, Main Beach, owned by Sunland Group, opened 2000 — [Commons Category:Palazzo Versace Australia](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Palazzo_Versace_Australia)

- From background knowledge, not verified this session: it was rebranded the Imperial Hotel Gold Coast around 2018. Palazzo Versace Dubai opened around 2015.
- Related: Versace Mansion (Casa Casuarina), Miami: [File:Versace mansion - entrance steps.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Versace_mansion_-_entrance_steps.jpg) (license: verify). This was Gianni Versace's own home, now a hotel, and is a "living" scene candidate.

### Inferences
- For "living", the contrast to make is that the most totalising interiors (Palazzo Versace, Versace Mansion) are the most logo-heavy. Armani/Casa and Fendi Château sell a brand "atmosphere" without the mark, which sits closer to the thesis's middle ground.
- The car-lift towers (Porsche, Bentley) are a vivid hybrid of the car and home scenes, since the branded car becomes part of the living room.

## 4. Branded office and workspace (scene: office)

### Takeaway
The clearest "logo-way office" is LV's monogram desk trunk lineage. It runs from the 1929 Stokowski secretary trunk to the Secrétaire Bureau 2.0 (relaunched for home working), and the current Secrétaire Artiste puts a whole desk inside Monogram canvas. LV x Singer's removable dashboard clock that becomes a desk clock links the car and office scenes.

### Cited Findings
- The Stokowski desk trunk was built for conductor Leopold Stokowski (1929). It is a Monogram-canvas case that opens into a desk with shelves, a typewriter recess and drawers — [Fashionphile listing](https://www.fashionphile.com/p/louis-vuitton-monogram-stokowski-secretary-desk-trunk-265480); [Magnifissance](https://magnifissance.com/print-edition/issue-107/louis-vuitton-bike-stokowski-trunk/)
- Secrétaire Bureau 2.0 was "reinvented for elevated remote working" for LV's 200th anniversary (2021). It has a collapsible table, a folding stool and five drawers — [Elite Traveler](https://elitetraveler.com/lifestyle/architecture-interiors/louis-vuitton-bureau-trunk)
- Secrétaire Artiste (Grace Coddington, Travel With collection) is based on the 1929 Stokowski. It is made in Monogram coated canvas with brass, 11 drawers and a collapsible table, measuring 52 × 107 × 57 cm — [Louis Vuitton UK product page M14600](https://uk.louisvuitton.com/eng-gb/products/secretaire-artiste-trunk-autres-toiles-monogram-nvprod6700121v/M14600)
- An antique 20th-century "Malle Secrétaire" in monogram canvas is listed at about £67k — [Vinterior](https://www.vinterior.co/furniture/storage/secretary-desks/antique-20thc-louis-vuitton-malle-secretaire-desk-trunk-in-monogram-canvas-sku81513011)
- The LV x Singer dashboard clock can be removed and used as a desk clock — [Europa Star](https://www.europastar.com/time-keeper/1004116343-louis-vuitton-takes-the-road-with-singer.html)
- Commons: there is an LV headquarters photo, [File:LOUIS VUITTON headquarters, Paris, France ( Ank Kumar, Infosys Limited ).jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:LOUIS_VUITTON_headquarters,_Paris,_France_(_Ank_Kumar,_Infosys_Limited_).jpg) (license: verify), which may stand in for a "brand office".

### Inferences
- The desk trunk is ideal for the "office" scene. It is literally a workplace folded inside the monogram, and its "remote working" relaunch puts the logo into the home office.

### Gaps
- No verified example of a fashion house designing a whole branded office interior for a client.

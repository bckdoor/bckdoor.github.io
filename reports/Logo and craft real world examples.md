# Two ways to dress a whole day

For every one of the eight scenes in one person's day, a real, citable object exists on each side. The "logo way" has a Louis Vuitton product, or a near-LV equivalent, at almost every stop: the Monogram Flower Tile tea set, the LV Trainer, the Speedy and Keepall, the Louis Vuitton x Singer Porsche 911, the Malle Secrétaire Artiste desk trunk, the monogram trunk wrapped around 103–111 avenue des Champs-Élysées, and the 2026 "Louis Vuitton Hotel" pop-ups whose rooms are named after bags. The "our way" side is just as complete, but it draws on many makers instead of one house, and in each case the identity sits in a material, a joint or a repair: a Nanbu iron kettle and a kintsugi chip repair, boro cloth held together by sashiko, a Goodyear welt built to be resoled, a vegetable-tanned Frank Clegg bag, Singer's woven-leather seats, Esherick's bolted oak stair and Vitsoe's 606 rail, Lewerentz's uncut bricks at Klippan, and Mogensen's buckled saddle leather. The most useful material is the **same-object pairs**. The clearest is the Singer-built Porsche 911 with LV Monogram flowers cut into its seat perforations, set against a standard Singer cabin whose woven leather and brass grommets carry the identity without any mark. The pairs show that the two ways differ in where identity is placed, not in quality of craft. That is also why the **Hermès Birkin** belongs in a boundary category and not on the "our way" side. Its craft is real, but waitlists, resale premiums and a dismissed tying lawsuit show that the market reads its silhouette as a logo. One caveat applies to everything below: **Wikimedia Commons could not be reached during research**. Every licence came from a search engine's reading of the file page and must be checked on that page. A free photo licence also covers only the photograph, not the design it shows.

## How to read the scene tables

Each scene has two tables. The logo-way table lists objects where the house mark itself is the surface, or where the house's "codes" stand in for it. The our-way table lists objects whose coherence comes from material, joint, repair or patina, with no visible logo or only an incidental small one. The photo column gives a Commons file with licence and author where the search returned them. Otherwise it points to an official or press page, which is for linking only, not for reuse. **"verify licence"** marks every licence the search did not return. Even entries marked "CC BY-SA 4.0" should be confirmed on the file page before use, because no Commons page was opened directly.

One precision runs through the logo side. Louis Vuitton itself tones the monogram down as objects get bigger. Most Objets Nomades furniture only *abstracts* the flower or diamond into a shape. Tokujin Yoshioka's Blossom Stool and Marcel Wanders's Diamond Screen are examples. The Raw Edges *Binda* sofa (Milan 2023) is not described as monogram-printed at all ([Luxuo](https://www.luxuo.com/culture/design/luxury-furniture-for-the-home-louis-vuitton-unveils-the-new-objets-nomades-collection-for-2017-in-milan.html); [Home Journal](https://www.homejournal.com/en/article/Louis-Vuitton-dazzles-with-11－piece-homeware,-furniture-and-lighting-collection-at-Milan-Design-Week-2023/)). The literal "logo everywhere" lives in the trunks, the small objects, the car and the pop-up environments. Fendi Casa follows the same pattern: its furniture is a "subtler take" on the house, with FF on textiles and boxes ([Harrods](https://www.harrods.com/en-hk/c/stories/home/fashion-home-brands)).

## Eight scenes, sixteen pairs

### 1. Morning tea and tableware: printed pattern versus a chip mended in gold

On the logo side, the identity is applied as decoration to standard porcelain. LV's first Art of Dining range put a watercolour **Monogram Flower Tile** on Limoges porcelain, tea set included ([Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/the-kurator/luxury/porcelain-perfection-louis-vuitton-unveils-opulent-tableware-collection-1.1700238110470)). LV also sells a Monogram-canvas trunk to keep it in, the **Malle Vaisselier**, listed at ₹1.55 crore, about US$185k by my conversion ([LV India M15365](https://in.louisvuitton.com/eng-in/products/malle-vaisselier-monogram-nvprod6340099v/M15365)). Versace's Medusa head has sat on Rosenthal porcelain since 1993 ([Rosenthal press](https://rosenthal.mynewsdesk.com/pressreleases/rosenthal-and-versace-celebrate-25-years-of-an-iconic-partnership-2399687)). On the craft side, the identity is built up through use. Kaikado caddies (Kyoto, since 1875) darken with handling, with copper changing in months and tin in years ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaikado); [Yoshien](https://www.yoshien.com/en/tea-caddy-kaikado-brass-100g.html)). A kintsugi chip repair photographed after a year of use shows repair and wear in one image.

### 2. Clothes: the weakest logo-side evidence, the strongest our-way photo

This is where the notes are thinnest on the logo side. **No source was retrieved for LV monogram ready-to-wear**, and the research flagged it as a gap (see the licences-and-gaps note at the end). The well-known logomania garments, such as the 2017 Supreme x Louis Vuitton collaboration and Dapper Dan's Harlem bootleg-monogram couture, were recorded only as background knowledge without citations. They should not be stated as fact until sourced. The best cited logo-side clothing evidence is indirect. The 2026 **Monogram Origine** collection re-created the 1896 pattern on linen-cotton canvas by pochoir stencilling ([Rain Magazine](https://rain-mag.com/louis-vuitton-marks-130-years-of-the-monogram-with-year-long-celebration)). *Succession*'s **$525 Loro Piana cap** shows the logo-light code doing a logo's job ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2023/07/24/stealth-wealth-clothing-succession)), which is covered under boundary cases. On the craft side the evidence is excellent. A **boro** quilt held together by sashiko is the single strongest "repair as coherence" image in the whole set: it has museum provenance, a clean licence, and a visible repair joint.

### 3. Shoes: the $1,200 trainer versus the last kept on a shelf

The LV Trainer by Virgil Abloh (SS19) sells in Monogram denim with LV Initials. A (RED) sample carried a Sotheby's estimate of **$30,000–40,000** ([Highsnobiety](https://cr-fe-eu.highsnobiety.com/p/virgil-abloh-louis-vuitton-1-trainer-sothebys-auction); [LV UK 1AJCKU](https://uk.louisvuitton.com/eng-gb/products/lv-trainer-nvprod6720009v/1AJCKU)). On the craft side, the identity is the person's foot, stored. John Lobb in St James's keeps an estimated **20,000–30,000** client lasts, Churchill's among them, so each later pair matches the first ([The Manufacturer](https://www.themanufacturer.com/articles/john-lobb-the-last-shall-be-first/)). Crockett & Jones rebuild welted shoes on their original last in Northampton ([Gentleman's Journal](https://www.thegentlemansjournal.com//article/heres-how-crockett-jones-give-your-shoes-a-new-lease-of-life/)). The Goodyear welt is the joint that makes this possible: the outsole is stitched to a welt rather than to the upper, so it can be replaced again and again ([USPTO 8336229](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/8336229)).

### 5. Car interior: the cleanest same-object pair in the set

**Louis Vuitton x Singer** is the clearest "same object, two ways" case. Two Porsche 911s reimagined by Singer (reported August 2025, year to confirm) have **Monogram flowers cut into the seat ventilation holes**. The trim is quilted after the malletage trunk lining, and the removable Tambour-style dashboard clock doubles as a desk clock ([CarExpert](https://www.carexpert.com.au/car-news/singer-and-louis-vuitton-reimagine-the-porsche-911-for-monterey-car-week); [Europa Star](https://www.europastar.com/time-keeper/1004116343-louis-vuitton-takes-the-road-with-singer.html)). A standard Singer cabin uses the same coachwork and the same craftspeople, but the identity is carried by woven leather inserts, brass grommets and wood or cork trim ([Classic Driver](https://www.classicdriver.com/en/node/1062669); [stuttcars](https://stuttcars.com/?p=479719)). The logo-side lineage goes back to the 1972–73 AMC Hornet "Gucci", a $141.75 trim package of which 4,835 were sold ([Hagerty](https://hagerty.com/media/?p=329702)). On the craft side, the cultural rule comes from the Pebble Beach and FIVA preservation classes, whose judges would rather see an original seat **repaired than replaced** ([Hagerty](https://hagerty.com/media/?p=3246)).

### 6. Office: a desk folded inside the monogram versus a rail that outlasts its books

LV's desk trunk lineage starts with the 1929 **Stokowski** secretary trunk. The **Secrétaire Bureau 2.0** was relaunched "for elevated remote working" for the 200th anniversary in 2021, and the current **Malle Secrétaire Artiste** (Grace Coddington) puts a collapsible desk and 11 drawers inside Monogram canvas ([Elite Traveler](https://elitetraveler.com/lifestyle/architecture-interiors/louis-vuitton-bureau-trunk); [LV UK M14600](https://uk.louisvuitton.com/eng-gb/products/secretaire-artiste-trunk-autres-toiles-monogram-nvprod6700121v/M14600)). On the craft side, the **Wharton Esherick Studio** was hand-built over 40 years. Its 1930 red-oak spiral stair leaves the bolts of its cantilevered treads exposed ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wharton_Esherick_Museum); [NHL nomination](https://npgallery.nps.gov/NRHP/GetAsset/NHLS/73001615_text)). **Vitsoe's 606** (Rams, 1960) is the system version: a precise rail-and-pin joint that holds whatever books arrive over decades. Scarpa's **Olivetti showroom** in Venice (1957–58) shows a company space whose identity is carried by marble, teak and stucco rather than by the logo ([Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Carlo_scarpa,_architect-_olivetti_showroom_stairs,_venice_1957-58_(33777096671).jpg)).

### 7. House and exterior: a building disguised as a trunk versus bricks that were never cut

The strongest single image of the logo way at architectural scale is **103–111 avenue des Champs-Élysées**. From late 2023 the whole building was hidden inside a wood-and-aluminium replica of a Monogram trunk while LV's first hotel was built behind it ([Domus](https://domusweb.it/en/news/2024/01/15/louis-vuitton-opens-its-first-hotel-in-paris-and-while-waiting-conceals-the-construction-site-inside-one-of-its-trunks.amp.html)). **Environmental groups and Paris elected officials challenged the permit in the administrative court as illegal advertising on a historic building**, and one report said it would stay until 2027 ([Le Bonbon](https://en.lebonbon.fr/paris/news/louis-vuitton-champs-elysees-bag/); [Luxus Plus](https://luxus-plus.com/en/champs-elysees-environmentalists-call-on-louis-vuitton-to-remove-its-giant-trunk/)). The court outcome, and whether the trunk was still there in October 2026, were not verified. Shanghai's **"The Louis"** (2025), a ship-shaped building about 30 m tall with a Monogram-patterned hull, is the permanent version ([FashionUnited](https://fashionunited.com/news/retail/louis-vuitton-presents-the-louis-in-shanghai/2025063066889)). On the craft side, **St Peter's, Klippan** (Lewerentz, 1962–66) follows one rule, never cut a brick, so the mortar joints are thick and irregular and the joint becomes the ornament ([ArchEyes](https://archeyes.com/?p=37830); [Caruso PDF](https://carusostjohn.com/media/files/Adam_Caruso_-_Sigurd_Lewerentz_and_a_Material_Basis_for_Form.pdf)). The **Bruder Klaus Field Chapel** (Zumthor, 2005–07) is rammed concrete around 112 spruce trunks that were later burned out, leaving a charred negative of the wood ([ArchDaily](https://www.archdaily.com/106352/bruder-klaus-field-chapel-peter-zumthor/)).

### 8. Living room and couch: a conversation sofa in Medusa versus a seat held by buckles

The most logo-saturated sofa found is **Versace Home's Medusa '95 Conversation Sofa**. It was shown at Palazzo Versace in Milan in April 2024 with Barocco chenille cushions, gold Medusa hardware nearby, and complete living, dining and sleeping rooms staged around it ([Luxury Living Group](https://luxurylivinggroup.com/blogs/news/versace-home-milano-design-week-2024)). LV's own sofa, Raw Edges' *Binda*, is not described as monogram-printed, so it works as an LV example of the non-logo way. On the craft side, **Mogensen's Spanish Chair** (1958) holds thick vegetable-tanned saddle leather to an oak frame with buckles ([Miliashop](https://miliashop.com/en/chair/19086-the-spanish-fredericia-chair.html)). **Bellini's Camaleonda** (1970, reissued 2020) gets its capitonné surface from a system of cables, hooks and rings that also lets the modules be unhooked and rearranged ([Galerie](https://galeriemagazine.com/bb-italia-mario-bellini-camaleonda-sofa/)). **De Sede's DS-600** (1972) joins its segments with a plug-in hinge and a zip, so the joint is the design ([Design-Market](https://www.design-market.us/240019-vintage-ds-600-non-stop-modular-sofa-in-fullblack-leather-by-de-sede-1972.html)).

## The whole life in one monogram already exists, as a pop-up

The strongest real-world example of "a whole life in one monogram" is not a celebrity house. No reputable press report of one was found, and none should be claimed. It is the **2026 Louis Vuitton Hotel** pop-ups for the Monogram's 130th anniversary. In New York (Soho) the visitor enters through the **Keepall Lobby**, passes the **Neverfull Gym**, and reaches the **Speedy Room 1930** decorated with Speedy bags ([Galerie](https://galeriemagazine.com/louis-vuitton-celebrates-130-years-of-its-iconic-monogram/)). Editions ran in Seoul, New York, Shanghai, Bangkok and London, the last at Berkeley Square, Mayfair, from 24 April to 21 June 2026 ([Esquire India](https://www.esquireindia.co.in/amp/story/style/just-landed/inside-louis-vuittons-fake-hotel-in-bangkok); [Hunger](https://hungermag.com/art-culture/louis-vuitton-has-opened-an-ephemeral-townhouse-hotel-in-mayfair); [FashionUnited India](https://fashionunited.in/news/retail/louis-vuitton-opens-temporary-hotel-in-london/2026042454178)). It is the house itself proposing a monogrammed domestic life, room by room, which makes it an honest image of the totalising Gesamtkunstwerk. The Champs-Élysées trunk is its architectural twin. In the challengers' framing, the building has *become* advertising, and that is exactly the totalising claim made visible in public.

## Boundary cases: real craft, read as a logo

**The Hermès Birkin is not an "our way" example**, even though it is hand-made. Each one is assembled by a single craftsman who signs it ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birkin_bag)). But access is managed through purchase history. The federal tying claim in *Cavalleri v. Hermès* alleged that buyers had to build up spending on other goods before being "offered" a Birkin. It was dismissed with prejudice on 17 September 2025, and an appeal to the Ninth Circuit was reportedly filed ([The Fashion Law](https://www.thefashionlaw.com/hermes-beats-antitrust-lawsuit-over-alleged-birkin-bag-allocation-scheme/); [Holland & Knight](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2025/10/hermes-bags-antitrust-win-that-clarifies-luxury-tying-claims)). Resale premiums of about **2.2× retail at the 2022 peak, compressing to 1.4–2× in late 2025** (reseller data), show the market pricing the silhouette as a mark ([The Luxury Closet](https://theluxurycloset.com/blog/2026/05/27/hermes-resale-value-2026-why-birkins-kellys-are-the-worlds-best-wearable-investment/)). Commons has well-licensed Birkin photos, for example [File:Hermes Birkin.JPG](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hermes_Birkin.JPG) (Theorb, public domain) and [File:Croc Birkin bag.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Croc_Birkin_bag.jpg) (Ohconfucius, CC BY 3.0). They are useful for illustrating this boundary, not for the craft side.

The **Bugatti Veyron 16.4 Fbg par Hermès** (Geneva 2008, €1.55m before tax) has inner surfaces sheathed in Hermès calfskin. Its "saddle-stitch" reference, though, is on the outside, in the air vents around the wheel rims, and the centre wheel locks carry an "H" ([Bugatti Newsroom](https://newsroom.bugatti.com/press-releases/the-bugatti-veyron-fbg-par-herm-s-)). It is a craft collaboration that still signs its work. **Maison Seoul** shows the opposite move: a logo house building logo-light, where the Gehry name and the sculptural form do the branding work ([Inside Retail Asia](https://insideretail.asia/2019/11/05/louis-vuitton-seoul-flagship-opens/)). **Quiet luxury** is the general case. *Succession*'s $525 Loro Piana cap made the absence of a logo into an insider code ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2023/07/24/stealth-wealth-clothing-succession)), and critics read it as exclusive signalling rather than modesty ([City AM](https://www.cityam.com/everyone-is-talking-about-quiet-luxury-but-theres-a-problem/)). The same drift turns intrecciato weave, Margiela's four stitches and the Tabi toe into logos once they circulate. Small marks on otherwise craft-led objects, such as Opinel's crowned hand, Leica's red dot and Barbour's badge, are minor. Leica marketing the M10-R Black Paint as designed to wear through to brass shows that patina itself can be styled and sold ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/news/stunning-leica-m-10r-black-paint-edition-is-designed-to-age-gracefully)).

## Counter-cases: when the total work freezes, is overwritten or is mocked

**Prada Marfa** (Elmgreen & Dragset, 2005) is the art-world critique of the boutique as logo-object. It is a sealed adobe-and-glass shop on US Route 90 near Valentine, Texas, with a door that does not open and windows showing Prada FW2005 shoes and bags chosen by Miuccia Prada, who permitted use of the trademark ([Category:Prada Marfa](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Prada_Marfa)). It has about 77 Commons files, such as [File:(Valentine, Texas) Prada Marfa.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:(Valentine,_Texas)_Prada_Marfa.jpg) (**verify licence**; Panoramio imports are usually CC BY-SA 3.0). The our-way side has its own failure modes. **Kettle's Yard** in Cambridge is a "harmonic whole" of art, furniture and pebbles that Jim Ede gave to the university in 1966. It is kept "by and large as Jim and Helen left it", a Gesamtkunstwerk frozen by bequest ([Kettle's Yard](https://www.kettlesyard.cam.ac.uk/history-of-kettles-yard/)). The free photo is exterior only: [File:Kettle's_Yard.JPG](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kettle's_Yard.JPG), CC BY-SA 4.0. **Villa E-1027** (Eileen Gray with Jean Badovici, 1926–29, historic monument since 2000) is the overwritten case. Le Corbusier later painted murals over Gray's interior without her consent. That fact is background knowledge and was not re-sourced. The only free image is [Sea_shore_at_Roquebrune.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sea_shore_at_Roquebrune.jpg) (Tangopaso, **verify licence**), with the villa behind trees. Together these show that removing the logo does not, by itself, prevent totalising. A craft whole can also be sealed against further life, or claimed by another author.

## A note on licences and gaps

**Wikimedia Commons and Wikipedia were not reachable from the research environment.** Both direct fetch and the API failed. Every Commons URL above came from a site-restricted web search, and every licence and author came from the search engine's reading of the file page. Entries given a licence above should still be opened and confirmed. Entries marked **verify licence** had no licence returned. Flickr-import filenames ending in a numeric ID are usually CC BY or CC BY-SA, but each must be checked. Logos and monogram patterns on Commons are often tagged as trademarks, and store photographs may carry freedom-of-panorama limits. **A free photo licence covers the photograph, not the design shown.** The LV monogram is a registered trademark, and furniture by Mogensen, Bellini, Perriand or Gray, along with buildings by Zumthor or Gehry, may still be protected even when the photograph is CC-licensed. Several facts also remain open: no source was retrieved for LV monogram ready-to-wear or for Supreme x LV; there is no free photo of a Singer interior, a Kaikado caddy, a Frank Clegg bag or the Champs-Élysées trunk; the LV x Singer reveal year and the Champs-Élysées court outcome are unconfirmed; and the Aoki monogram facade's store name is unconfirmed.

## Conclusion

Mapping the day scene by scene shows that the logo way is easiest to document and hardest to photograph freely. LV owns the images of its tea set, trainer, desk trunk, car and hotel, so a thesis that shows them will depend on press pages. The craft way is easier to illustrate under free licences, because museums, Flickr photographers and HABS have recorded boro, Klippan, Esherick and the Citroën DS for decades. That asymmetry reflects the argument itself: identity that sits in a trademark is controlled, while identity that sits in a joint or a patina can be photographed by anyone.

The pairs also sharpen what "without totalising" has to mean. LV x Singer and the plain Singer share a builder, and LV's own Binda sofa carries no monogram, so the difference is neither craft quality nor even brand. It is whether one author's mark is allowed to claim every surface. The Birkin, quiet luxury, Kettle's Yard and E-1027 show that removing the mark is not enough. Craft can be read as a logo, and a craft whole can be frozen or overwritten. The defensible "our way" is the version whose joints stay open to repair and whose surfaces record the owner's use rather than the maker's signature.
